Overview

This Privacy Policy explains how Party Pic Bingo handles information. Party Pic Bingo is an iPhone party game where players complete bingo tiles by taking or choosing photos for party challenges.

The app is built to run locally on your device. It does not require an account, login, internet connection, or server-side service to play.

Information We Collect

We do not collect personal information through Party Pic Bingo. The app does not collect names, email addresses, phone numbers, contacts, precise location, device identifiers, advertising identifiers, analytics events, or usage profiles.

The app does not include third-party advertising SDKs, analytics SDKs, tracking SDKs, or social login tools.

Photos and Game Data

Photos, bingo boards, challenge progress, custom challenges, custom themes, and game history are stored locally on your device using Apple-provided storage frameworks.

  • Photos are saved in the app's local storage and are not uploaded to us.
  • Challenge photos are re-encoded by the app, which removes original camera and location metadata from the stored app copy.
  • Like other local app data, this information may be included in your device backups depending on your Apple and iCloud settings.

Permissions

Party Pic Bingo may ask for the following iOS permissions only when needed:

  • Camera: used when you choose to take a photo for a bingo challenge.
  • Photo Library: used when you choose an existing image for a challenge through Apple's photo picker.
  • Add to Photo Library: used only if you choose to save a shared board image to your photo library.

You can manage these permissions at any time in the iOS Settings app.

Sharing

Party Pic Bingo uses the native iOS share sheet when you choose to share a board or photo. Sharing is always initiated by you. We do not automatically share your photos or game data.

Once you share content to another app or person, that content is handled by the destination you selected and is subject to that destination's privacy practices.

Optional 21+ Party Mode

Party Pic Bingo includes an optional 21+ Party Night setting with adults-only party prompts. This setting is stored locally on your device. The app does not collect age, identity, or verification information.

Children

Party Pic Bingo does not knowingly collect personal information from children. Because the app does not collect personal information from any users, there is no child personal information for us to sell, share, or process.

Retention and Deletion

Local game data remains on your device until you delete it. You can delete individual games inside the app. Deleting a game removes the photos associated with that game from the app's local storage.

Deleting Party Pic Bingo from your device removes the app and its local data. Backups managed by Apple may retain copies according to your Apple and iCloud settings.

Security

Party Pic Bingo relies on Apple's operating system protections for local app storage and permissions. The app does not implement custom encryption or transmit app data to a server.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy if the app's features or data practices change. The effective date at the top of this page will be updated when changes are made.
