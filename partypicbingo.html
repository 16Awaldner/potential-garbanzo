Overview

This Privacy Policy explains how Party Pic Bingo handles information. Party Pic Bingo is an iPhone and iPad party game where players complete bingo tiles by taking or choosing photos for party challenges.

The app runs locally on your device. It does not require an account, login, internet connection, or server-side service to play.

Information We Collect

We do not collect personal information through Party Pic Bingo. Nothing you do in the app is sent to us. We do not collect email addresses, phone numbers, contacts, location, device identifiers, advertising identifiers, analytics events, or usage profiles.

The app does not include third-party advertising SDKs, analytics SDKs, tracking SDKs, or social login tools, and it has no in-app purchases.

Photos and Game Data

The following is stored locally on your device:

  • Challenge photos and thumbnails, bingo boards, challenge progress, custom challenges, custom themes, and game history.
  • Your settings, including the optional player name you type for Party Race and whether 21+ mode is on.
  • Photos are re-encoded by the app, which removes original camera and location metadata from the stored copy.
  • Each photo is developed with the game's film (such as Disposable or Instant) on your device before it is saved. The app keeps only this filtered copy, not your original.
  • Every saved photo carries a small visible “PARTY PIC BINGO” mark. On Disposable film, the default, the mark also includes a date stamp showing the day the photo was added to the board, which for a photo chosen from your library may differ from the day it was taken. The mark stays on any photo you share, frame, use in a recap video, or enter in Best Shot.
  • Like other local app data, this information may be included in your device backups depending on your Apple and iCloud settings.

Permissions

Party Pic Bingo asks for iOS permissions only when you use a feature that needs them:

  • Camera: used when you take a photo for a bingo challenge.
  • Local Network: used only when you start or join a Party Race, so your device can find other players nearby.
  • Add to Photo Library: used only if you choose to save a board image, framed photo, challenge photo, or recap video to your photo library.

To pick an existing photo, the app uses Apple's photo picker, which gives the app only the photo you choose and does not grant access to your library. You can manage permissions at any time in the iOS Settings app.

Party Race (Nearby Play)

Party Race is optional. When you start or join one, the app uses Apple's Multipeer Connectivity to connect directly to nearby iPhones and iPads over Wi-Fi or Bluetooth. Connections are encrypted, and nothing passes through the internet or our servers.

  • While searching for a race, your device advertises the player name you entered and the race code. Other nearby devices running Party Pic Bingo may be able to see these even if they are not in your race.
  • While you are in a race, your name, board name, and progress (squares completed, squares left to bingo, and when you got bingo) are sent to the other players automatically each time your game changes.
  • Best Shot: a photo is shared only if you choose to enter it in the Best Shot vote. It is sent with your name and the challenge title. Votes are shared with the same players.
  • Other players' devices display this information during the race and do not save it.
  • Sharing stops when you leave the race, delete that game, or join a different race.

Use a first name or nickname you're comfortable showing to people nearby.

Sharing

Other than Party Race, content leaves your device only when you choose to share it through the iOS share sheet, a QR code, or by copying it. You can share:

  • Board images and photos: a board image, framed photos, or a package that includes every challenge photo from a game. Framed photos show the challenge title, square number, date the square was completed, and the Party Pic Bingo name.
  • Recap videos: made from a game's photos, name, theme, and date, with each photo's challenge title.
  • Board links and board files: the board name, challenge text, themes, game settings, film and photo frame style, party code, and blitz length, so others can play the same board.
  • Host invites (QR code): your game's name, theme, settings, film and photo frame style, party code, and blitz end time.
  • Challenge packs: the text of the challenges you choose.

The app reads your clipboard only when you tap Paste from Clipboard to import a board link or challenge pack. Imported challenges are added to your challenge bank.

Once you share content to another app or person, it is handled by the destination you selected and is subject to that destination's privacy practices.

On-Device Features

  • Photo referee: optionally checks a photo using Apple's on-device Vision framework, including counting faces in the picture. It does not identify anyone, and the result is not saved or sent anywhere.
  • Widget and Live Activity: show your current board's name, theme, progress, and timer on your Home Screen, Lock Screen, and Dynamic Island. This information is shared only between the app and its widget on your device, and is visible to anyone who can see your screen.

Optional 21+ Party Mode

Party Pic Bingo includes an optional 21+ Party Night setting with adults-only party prompts. Turning it on asks you to confirm you are 21 or older. Only the on/off setting is stored, locally on your device. The app does not collect age, identity, or verification information.

Children

Party Pic Bingo does not knowingly collect personal information from children, and we do not collect personal information from any users. Because Party Race shares a typed name and chosen photos with nearby players, parents should supervise children who use it.

Retention and Deletion

Local game data remains on your device until you delete it:

  • Clearing a square deletes its photo.
  • Deleting a game removes that game and its photos from the app's storage.
  • Delete All Game History in Settings removes every game and its photos.
  • Your player name and settings can be changed or cleared in Settings.

Temporary copies made for sharing are cleared by iOS over time. Deleting Party Pic Bingo removes the app and all of its local data. Backups managed by Apple may retain copies according to your Apple and iCloud settings. We cannot delete content you have already shared with others.

Security

Party Pic Bingo relies on Apple's operating system protections for local app storage and permissions. Party Race connections between devices are encrypted. The app does not transmit data to any server.
