Heirloom Kitchen Privacy Policy

Privacy Policy for Heirloom Kitchen

Effective Date: November 19, 2026

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use the Heirloom Kitchen mobile application (“the App”). In short: the App does not connect to the internet. Your recipes, recordings, photos and stories stay on your device, and nothing leaves it unless you share a copy yourself.

2. Information We Collect

We, the developer, do not collect or receive any personal information from you.

  • No accounts or sign-in
  • No developer servers, and no network connections. The App never sends anything to us or anyone else.
  • No analytics, crash reporting, advertising or tracking
  • No third-party code libraries that collect data

3. Information Stored on Your Device

To work, the App saves the following only on your device:

  • Recipes: ingredients, family measurements, steps, notes, stories, story-interview answers, holiday menus, shopping lists and shows you create
  • Videos, photos and audio you record or add, including pictures of dishes, scans of handwritten recipe cards and music you choose for shows
  • Transcripts: editable text the App makes from a recording or a recipe card when you ask it to
  • Family cook profiles: the name, relationship and portrait photo you enter for each cook
  • Shared copies waiting to be sent (PDFs, movies and archives), and safety copies the App keeps when an imported recipe replaces one you already had
  • Preferences, such as recording settings and whether you have seen the welcome

Photos and videos you add from your library or Files are kept as the original files. If your camera saved a location or date inside a photo, that information stays in the App’s copy and in any copy you share. Clips recorded in the App do not add a location.

None of this is sent to us. If you use iCloud Backup or back up your device to a computer, your backup may include this data, according to your Apple settings.

4. Camera, Microphone, Speech and Photos

The App asks for permission before using each of these, and uses them only for features you start:

  • Camera: to record the cook’s demonstrations and photograph dishes and recipe cards.
  • Microphone: to record the cook’s voice with a clip, and to hear hands-free Cook Mode commands like “next step”. Hands-free commands are not recorded or saved.
  • Speech recognition: to turn a recorded clip into editable words and to understand hands-free commands. The App requires Apple’s on-device speech recognition, so your audio is never sent to a server.
  • Text recognition: reading a scanned recipe card into text also happens on your device.
  • Photos and files: you can pick photos or videos with Apple’s photo picker, or files with the Files picker, which share only the items you choose. The App asks to add to your photo library only when you save a cooking movie there.
  • Notifications: Cook Mode kitchen timers can alert you when they finish. These are local notifications scheduled on your device, not sent from a server.

You can change any of these permissions at any time in iOS Settings › Heirloom Kitchen.

5. Sharing and Exporting

When you share a recipe, keepsake book PDF, movie or .heirloom archive, or play a family screening over AirPlay, the App makes a copy and hands it to the destination you choose, such as Messages, Mail, AirDrop, Files or your TV. What happens to that copy afterwards is up to you and the service you send it to. A shared recipe can include the cook’s name, photos, videos and stories, so share it only with people you trust.

When you open a .heirloom archive someone sends you, the App reads it on your device and adds it to your recipe box.

6. Children’s Privacy

The App does not collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13. Recipes and recordings of family members, including children, stay on the device where they were made unless you share them.

7. Keeping and Deleting Your Data

  • A recipe: delete it from the recipe page or the recipe box. This permanently removes the recipe and its clips and photos.
  • A clip or transcript: remove a clip from a recipe, or delete transcript words, while editing the recipe. Leftover clips can be permanently deleted in Settings › Recover clips.
  • A family cook: swipe the cook in Settings › Family cooks to delete their profile.
  • Shared copies and safety copies: remove them in Settings under Storage and Safety copies.
  • Example recipes: remove them in Settings › Examples.
  • Everything: deleting the App removes all of its data from your device. Device backups are kept according to your Apple settings, and copies you shared are not affected.

8. Your Privacy Rights

California residents (CCPA/CPRA): you may have the right to know what personal information is collected, request deletion, and opt out of the sale or sharing of personal information, subject to applicable law.

EEA and UK users (GDPR): you may have rights including access, correction, deletion, and restriction of processing, subject to applicable law.

We do not hold, sell or share any personal information about you, because the App’s data stays on your device, where you control it. You can contact us with any question.

9. Data Security

Your App data is protected by the security built into iOS. Because the App has no servers and makes no network connections, there is no developer database of your information that could be breached.

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated Effective Date.
