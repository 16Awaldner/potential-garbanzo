01The short version

Unscripted (formerly Party Confessional) collects no data. There are no servers, accounts, analytics, ads or tracking, and no third-party code. What you and your guests record stays on your devices. It leaves them only when you share it, or when you choose to connect a roaming camera to your booth.

The developer never receives, sees or stores your recordings or anything else from the app. The app never connects to the internet.

02What the app keeps on your device

To work, the app saves these on the device it runs on, inside its own storage:

  • Events: names, dates, types, themes, settings and the questions you use.
  • Recordings: the videos (with sound) and photos recorded at the booth, sent by a roaming camera or imported from Photos.
  • Details about each recording: the time, the question asked, the name and caption a guest typed (if any), whether they agreed to the on-camera notice, a transcript if you made one, and your own notes, tags and edits.
  • Secret missions: the guest’s name, the dare they were given, and whether they came back to confess.
  • Episodes: the most recent episode you rendered, which can include guests’ names, subtitles and music.
  • Music: a copy of any song you pick for the episode.
  • Your host PIN: stored in the iOS Keychain, salted and hashed, never in plain text.
  • Small app settings: such as whether you’ve seen the welcome guide and where the question rotation is up to. On a roaming camera, this includes the name you typed and the event name and code of the booth you last joined.

None of this is sent to the developer or to any server. Your files can be reached in Finder when the device is connected to a Mac. Like any app data, it may be included in your device’s own iCloud or computer backups if you have those turned on; those are governed by Apple’s privacy policy.

03Permissions and why

You can change these at any time in Settings › Unscripted.

  • Camera and microphone: to record when someone taps record at the booth or on a roaming camera. Nothing is recorded otherwise.
  • Photos (add only): to save the clips or episode you choose to your Photos library. The app can’t read your library with this permission.
  • Photo picker: to import videos and photos. It’s Apple’s picker: the app only receives the items you select and never has access to the rest of your library. Imported items may keep their original details, such as where they were taken, and those details may go with them if you share them later.
  • Speech Recognition: only if you turn on subtitles or tap Transcribe. Apple’s speech recognizer turns what’s said in a clip into text, on the device. Unscripted requires on-device recognition, so audio is never sent to Apple or anyone else; if your device can’t transcribe offline, the app says so instead.
  • Files: only when you pick a song for your episode. A copy is stored with the event, and the app sees only the file you choose.
  • Face ID, Touch ID or passcode: to unlock the booth. Apple handles this; the app only learns whether it succeeded and never receives biometric data.
  • Local Network: only for the optional roaming camera, below.

04Roaming camera

The roaming camera lets a second iPhone send the videos and photos it takes to your booth. It uses Apple’s MultipeerConnectivity to connect the two devices directly, over Wi-Fi or Bluetooth. The connection is encrypted and never uses the internet or any server.

  • The booth only accepts a phone that enters the event’s 6-digit code, and only while the event’s dashboard or booth is open. Making a new code disconnects every phone.
  • Once a phone has joined, each clip it records is sent to the booth automatically, with the time it was taken and the name the person filming typed in. The phone reconnects on its own to a booth it has joined before.
  • The booth sends back only the event’s name and whether each clip was saved.
  • The phone keeps each clip until the booth confirms it, then deletes its copy.
  • While the booth is accepting cameras, nearby devices may be able to see the event’s name, and a joining phone shows up nearby as “[name]’s camera”. Nobody can join or see anything else without the code.

05When recordings leave your device

Apart from the roaming camera, only when you choose to: through the share sheet (AirDrop, Messages, Mail, Files and so on), by saving to Photos, or by copying the files to a computer. Shared files are named with the guest’s name. Exporting a whole event folder also includes a list of each clip’s details, such as guest names, questions, notes, tags and whether the guest agreed to be recorded. Once shared, the recipient app or person’s own privacy terms apply.

Booth Mode is designed so guests cannot view, delete or export anyone’s recordings.

06For hosts: your guests

By default, before recording at the booth, each guest sees a notice that their recording is saved for the host to watch, edit and share, and recording starts only if they agree. The app notes on each clip whether the guest agreed. The host can turn this notice off in Event Settings. Clips from a roaming camera and items imported from Photos don’t show this notice and are marked as not agreed.

The host decides how the app is used and is responsible for the recordings made with it. Please let people know they are being recorded, especially by a roaming camera, get any consent required where you live, and respect requests to delete a recording.

If you were recorded and want a clip removed, ask the host: they can delete any clip in the app. The developer has no copy and no way to access it.

07Children

Unscripted isn’t directed at children under 13, and the developer doesn’t knowingly collect personal information from anyone, because it collects none at all. Hosts are responsible for getting a parent or guardian’s permission before filming children at their events.

08Deleting your data

  • Delete a clip from its screen in the Media Library. This also clears that event’s exported copies and rendered episode in the app.
  • Delete a whole event, with all its media, secret missions and music, in Event Settings › Delete Event.
  • On a roaming camera, clips that haven’t reached a booth stay on the phone until they send; deleting the app removes them.
  • Deleting the app removes everything it stored on that device. Copies you already shared or saved to Photos, and device backups, aren’t affected.

iOS may keep the Keychain entry holding the hashed host PIN after the app is deleted. It contains no personal information.

09Security

Your data is protected by the security built into iOS, including encryption of app data when the device has a passcode set. Roaming camera connections are encrypted and require the event code. Because nothing is sent over the internet, there is no server for anyone to breach. Keep your device protected with a passcode, and use Guided Access when the booth is unattended.

10Your rights

Depending on where you live, laws such as the GDPR or the CCPA may give you rights to access, correct or delete personal information a company holds about you. The developer holds no personal information about you, so there is nothing to provide or delete. If you have a question about your rights, please contact us.
