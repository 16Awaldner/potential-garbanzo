Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 16, 2026

Short version: Leveling keeps everything on your device. There are no accounts, no developer servers, no ads, and no analytics. We never receive your profile, photos, or activity unless you choose to send them to us.

Adults 21+ only: Leveling includes alcohol-related quests and is intended only for users who are 21 or older.

Entertainment purposes only: Use of the app is at your own discretion and responsibility.

1. Information We Collect

We do not collect personal information. The app has no account or login, makes no network connections, and does not send any data to us or to third parties.

The only way we receive information from you is if you choose to contact us, for example with Send Feedback in Settings. That opens your own email app; if you send the message, we receive your email address and whatever you write, and use it only to reply.

2. Information Stored on Your Device

To work, the app saves the following only on your device:

  • Profile: the name, job or profession, and date of birth you enter during setup, plus an optional profile photo
  • Age confirmation: the age you confirm on first launch and that you accepted the disclaimer
  • Gameplay: level, XP, gold, stats, quests, cosmetics, and inventory items (including any names or notes you add)
  • Activity history: a timestamped log of what you complete, including drinks you log (such as beers and shots)
  • Settings: haptics, daily reminder on/off and time, and basic app state such as a launch count

None of this is sent to us. If you use iCloud Backup or back up your device to a computer, your backup may include this data, according to your Apple settings.

3. Permissions

The app asks for permission only when you use a feature that needs it. You can change these at any time in the iOS Settings app.

  • Camera: used only if you choose to take a profile picture.
  • Photos: you can pick a profile picture with Apple’s photo picker, which shares only the photo you choose. The app cannot see the rest of your library.
  • Notifications: requested only if you turn on the daily reminder. Reminders are scheduled on your device; no push-notification service is used.

4. Backups and Sharing

Export Backup in Settings creates a file containing your profile (name, job, date of birth, and profile photo) and progress, and opens the iOS share sheet. The file goes only where you send it, and is then handled by that app or service under its own privacy policy. Import Backup reads a backup file you choose and stays on your device.

5. Third-Party Services

Leveling does not use any third-party analytics, advertising, tracking, crash-reporting, or account services, and it contains no in-app purchases.

6. Age Requirement and Children’s Privacy

Leveling is intended only for adults 21 and older. On first launch you must confirm you are 21 or older and accept a disclaimer. This is a self-declaration stored on your device; we do not verify it. The app is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 13 (or from anyone at all).

7. Keeping and Deleting Your Data

  • Individual items: quests, inventory items, and activity entries can be deleted in the app.
  • Start Over: in Settings, this deletes your profile, progress, and history and cancels reminders.
  • Everything: deleting the app removes all of its data from your device. Backups you exported or device backups are not affected.

8. Data Security

Your data is protected by the security built into iOS, including encryption when your device has a passcode. Because we do not maintain accounts or servers, there is no developer database of your information that could be breached.
