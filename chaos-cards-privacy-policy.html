Chaos Cards Privacy Policy

Privacy Policy for Chaos Cards

Effective Date: November 18, 2026

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use the Chaos Cards mobile application (“the App”). In short: everything you type into the App stays on your device, the App shows ads from Google AdMob, and in-app purchases are handled by Apple.

2. Information We Collect

We, the developer, do not collect or receive personal information from you.

  • No accounts or login
  • No developer servers. The App never sends your game data to us.
  • No analytics or crash-reporting tools of our own

3. Information Stored on Your Device

To work, the App saves the following only on your device:

  • Player names you enter, remembered for your next game
  • Game history: player names, the winner, high score, pack played, number of rounds, and the date
  • Custom packs made with Pack Builder: pack title, icon, and the card text you write
  • Purchase status: which packs and features you have unlocked
  • Preferences, such as haptics, whether you have seen the intro, and whether 18+ packs are shown

None of this is sent to us or to advertising partners. If you use iCloud Backup or back up your device to a computer, your backup may include this data, according to your Apple settings.

4. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The App shows a banner ad and a full-screen ad before games, using Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads SDK). Buying Remove Ads turns off all ads, and the App then never asks about ad consent or tracking.

Google and its partners may automatically collect information to serve ads, such as:

  • Device information (model, operating system, settings) and device identifiers
  • IP address and approximate location derived from it
  • Ad interactions, such as ads shown and tapped
  • Diagnostic data, such as crash and performance information

Google may use this information to show and measure ads, prevent fraud, and improve its services. Your player names, game history, and custom cards are not shared with Google.

Learn more in Google’s Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy and in how Google uses information from apps that use its services: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

5. Tracking and Ad Choices

Tracking permission: the App asks for permission through Apple’s App Tracking Transparency before showing ads. If you tap Allow, Google can use your device’s advertising identifier (IDFA) to show more relevant ads and measure them across apps and websites. If you tap Ask App Not to Track, it can’t, and you will still see ads, which may be less relevant. You can change this at any time in iOS Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking.

Consent form: in regions where the law requires it, such as the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom, Google’s consent form is shown before any ads load, so you can choose how your data is used for ads. You can change your choices at any time in the App under Settings › Ad privacy choices.

6. In-App Purchases

Card packs, Pack Builder, and Remove Ads are one-time purchases processed by Apple through the App Store. Apple handles payment, and we never receive your payment details. The App checks with Apple to confirm which items you own, including when you tap Restore Purchases. Apple’s handling of your purchase is covered by Apple’s privacy policy.

7. 18+ Content

Some packs contain adult content and are hidden by default. To show them, you turn on “Show 18+ packs” in Settings and confirm you are 18 or older. The App stores only an on/off setting on your device. It does not collect your age, identity, or any verification information.

8. Children’s Privacy

Chaos Cards is a party game for teens and adults and is not intended for children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child’s information was collected by a third-party service connected with the App, please contact us and we will review the issue.

9. Keeping and Deleting Your Data

  • Game history: swipe a game in History to delete it.
  • Everything else: deleting the App removes all of its data from your device. Device backups are kept according to your Apple settings.
  • Advertising data: Google keeps data it collects under its own policies.

10. Your Privacy Rights

California residents (CCPA/CPRA): you may have the right to know what personal information is collected, request deletion, and opt out of the sale or sharing of personal information, subject to applicable law.

EEA and UK users (GDPR): you may have rights including access, correction, deletion, and restriction of processing, subject to applicable law.

We do not hold personal information about you, because App data stays on your device, where you control it. You can limit advertising use of your data with the tracking setting and ad privacy choices described above. Requests about advertising data should go to Google using the links above. You can also contact us with any question.

11. Data Security

Your App data is protected by the security built into iOS. Because we do not run servers for the App, there is no developer database of your information that could be breached.

12. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated Effective Date.
