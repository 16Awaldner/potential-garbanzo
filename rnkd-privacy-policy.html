Common questions

Why can’t players find my room?

Keep the lobby open, make sure everyone allowed Local Network access, and use the same Wi‑Fi or turn on Wi‑Fi and Bluetooth on every device. Some public and guest networks block devices from seeing each other. You can turn Local Network access back on in iOS Settings › RNKD.

Do guests need to buy the pack I’m hosting?

No. Only the host needs to own a premium pack. Guests can play it in that room but don’t keep it.

How do I get my purchases on a new device?

Sign in with the same Apple Account and tap Restore Purchases on the Packs screen.

Can I move my player account to another device?

No. Player accounts are stored only on the device where they were created, with no cloud sync. Purchases do move: use Restore Purchases.

How do I delete my account?

Open Settings › Player Account › Delete Account. Deletion is immediate.

A fact in a pack looks wrong

Email us with the pack and the card. Corrections ship in an app update.

Can I suggest a mode or a pack?

Please do. Email us. We read every message.

RNKD Privacy Policy

Privacy Policy for RNKD

Effective Date: November 20, 2026

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use RNKD, a party game for iPhone and iPad (“the App”). One player hosts a room and everyone else joins from nearby devices on the same network.

The short version:

  • RNKD has no account server and no RNKD database. Your player profile, scores, and match history stay on your device.
  • When you play, your display name, avatar, and game moves go directly to the other devices in the room over an encrypted connection, not to us.
  • Apple handles purchases. We never see your payment details.
  • Google shows ads. RNKD asks for non-personalized ads, does not track you across apps, and never asks for your advertising identifier.
  • RNKD is designed for people aged 16 and older.

2. Information We Collect

We, the developer, do not collect or receive personal information from you.

  • No sign-in with email, phone number, or a third-party account. Player accounts are local profiles on your device.
  • No developer servers. The App never sends your profile, stats, or game data to us.
  • No analytics, crash reporting, or cloud sync of our own.

3. Information Stored on Your Device

To work, the App saves the following only on your device:

  • Player profile: the display name, avatar, and colour you choose, and a random identifier that keeps profiles on the device apart
  • Age range you confirm (for example “16–17” or “18+”). The App never asks for or stores your date of birth.
  • Stats: match history, scores, badges, streaks, and daily goals
  • Purchase status: which packs and whether Remove Ads your Apple Account owns, as reported by Apple
  • Settings and preferences

We cannot see or access any of this. If you use iCloud Backup or back up your device to a computer, your backup may include this data, according to your Apple settings.

4. Playing with People Nearby

RNKD connects directly to nearby devices on your local network. iOS asks your permission the first time you host or join a room. While you are in a room:

  • Your display name, avatar, colour, ready status, and game moves (rankings, votes, picks, and guesses) are sent to the other devices in that room.
  • If you turn on “Let hosts remember me”, a resettable random identifier is included so a host’s device can recognise you in a later game. You can reset it or turn it off in your player account settings.
  • The host’s device keeps the match result, and each player’s device keeps its own copy of the results it was shown. We cannot delete copies kept on other people’s devices.
  • Connections are encrypted and joined with a room code. Your age range, purchases, and stats are never sent to other players.

5. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The App shows banner ads and occasional full-screen ads between matches using Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads SDK) and Google’s User Messaging Platform for consent. Buying Remove Ads turns off these ads for your Apple Account.

RNKD sets up ads so that:

  • ads are non-personalized;
  • RNKD does not ask for App Tracking Transparency permission and does not access your device’s advertising identifier (IDFA);
  • ad content is limited to what is suitable for a teen audience, and consent requests are marked for the protective treatment given to younger users.

Even with non-personalized ads, Google and its partners may automatically process information to deliver ads, such as:

  • Device information (model, operating system, settings) and identifiers limited to the App or developer
  • IP address and approximate location derived from it
  • Ad interactions, such as ads shown and tapped, and other product-interaction data
  • Diagnostic data, such as crash and performance information

Google may use this information to show and measure ads, prevent fraud, and improve its services. Your player profile, age range, stats, and game moves are not shared with Google.

Learn more in Google’s Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy and in how Google uses information from apps that use its services: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

6. Ad Choices

In regions where the law requires it, such as the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, and Switzerland, Google’s consent form is shown before any ads load, so you can choose how your data is used for ads. You can change your choices at any time in the App under Settings › Privacy Choices.

7. In-App Purchases

Premium packs and Remove Ads are one-time purchases processed by Apple through the App Store. Apple handles payment, and we never receive your payment details. The App checks with Apple, on your device, which items you own, including when you tap Restore Purchases. Apple’s handling of your purchase is covered by Apple’s privacy policy: https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/

8. Sharing Results

If you choose to share a match recap, the App creates an image on your device and hands it to the iOS share sheet. What happens next depends on the app you share it with.

9. What RNKD Does Not Collect

RNKD does not collect your email address, phone number, contacts, precise location, photos, camera, microphone audio, or date of birth.

10. Children and Teenagers

RNKD is designed for people aged 16 and older. If the age step shows a player is under 16, the App explains that RNKD is not for them and does not let them create a player account. Players aged 16–17 receive teen-appropriate ad treatment. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child under 16 is using RNKD, please contact us.

11. Keeping and Deleting Your Data

  • Player account: delete it any time in Settings › Player Account › Delete Account. Deletion is immediate.
  • Everything else: deleting the App removes everything RNKD stored on your device. Device backups are kept according to your Apple settings.
  • Purchases stay with your Apple Account and can be restored.
  • Advertising data: Google keeps data it collects under its own policies.

12. Your Privacy Rights

California residents (CCPA/CPRA): you may have the right to know what personal information is collected, request deletion, and opt out of the sale or sharing of personal information, subject to applicable law. We do not sell or share your personal information.

EEA, UK, and Swiss users (GDPR): you may have rights including access, correction, deletion, restriction of processing, and the right to complain to your local data protection authority, subject to applicable law. Where Google processes data for ads, it does so based on the choices you make in the consent form.

We do not hold personal information about you, because App data stays on your device, where you can see, change, and delete it. Requests about advertising data should go to Google using the links above. You can also contact us with any privacy question.

13. Data Security

Your App data is protected by the security built into iOS, and games between devices are encrypted. Because we do not run servers for the App, there is no developer database of your information that could be breached.

14. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated Effective Date, and for significant changes we will tell you in the App.
